Why do we have a Verification Process?

At SmartIntern, the security and trust of our students are our top priorities. We manually vet every company that joins our platform.

  • ✅ CIN Verification: We cross-check your Company Identification Number to ensure legitimacy.
  • ✅ Quality Control: We ensure all posted internships offer genuine learning experiences.
  • ✅ Spam Prevention: Keeping the platform clean from fake listings guarantees high engagement from top-tier students.

Note: The approval process typically takes 24-48 hours. You will receive an email once your account is activated to post jobs.
